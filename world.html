World Building

The world of Echoes of Azure Cloud is divided into Three Realms.

  • Light realm:Home of weaker cultivators and it is the realm with the lowest amount of spritual energy.
  • Sovereign Realm: Domain of Celestial Clans and god-tier cultivators the gods.
  • Heavens Realm: It is also known as Graveyard of Gods its contains Celestial Peaks resting place for gods

In the Sovereign Realm there are 10 Sects stronger than any others.

  • 1.Azure Cloud Sect: Once the strongest clan now became weakest.
  • 2.Heaven Devourring Sect: The clan which trains ancient void techniques (antagonist clan).
  • 3.Thunderflame Sect: This clan practices both flame and thunder techniques it is said that this clan,s ansestor created a relam that contains the core of flame and thunder attributes.
  • 4.Jiulong Sect: The clan of martial gods practicing ancient god martial techniques.
  • 5.Radiant Moon Sect: The legend has it that this clan was build where the moonlight godess first descended on the realm.
  • 6.Ying Sect: The sect which lives in shadows and is home to the God of Death and Underworld.
  • 7.Ling Sect: Though it is considered as a celestial clan its the weakest among the lot Their ansestor is the God of Lightning.
  • 8.Zajio Sect: The home of hybrid cultivators found in depts of sacred forests.
  • 9.Jian Sect: The heaven of swordsmanship The home of ancient sword techniques.
  • 10.Shen Sect: The clan which trains spiritual techniques The most mysterious clan.
